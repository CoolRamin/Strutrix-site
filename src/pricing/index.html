Questions

Before you ask.

Do I need an account to use Strutrix Studio?

No. The model, the loads and combinations, the analysis, the steel design, the calculation report and model files on your own computer need no account and work offline. You sign in only for the cloud and the Pro features.

Is the free version a trial?

No. The free capabilities are not time-limited: nothing counts down and there is nothing to cancel. Only the Pro features have a free trial.

When will it be available?

Strutrix Studio is being prepared for the Microsoft Store and will be listed there soon. Write to us to be told the day it arrives.

What happens when the free trial ends?

Nothing is charged, and you are not asked for payment details at any point. The Pro features close, everything free keeps working exactly as before, and your cloud models stay in your account. A model that already holds a tapered tube still opens and runs; making a new one needs Pro.

Why is a model with a tapered tube saved to the cloud and not to a file?

A model that holds Pro content, a tapered tube, is saved to the cloud only, and the program says so when you save it. A model without one saves to a file on your computer as always. The exports and your firm on the report leave nothing in the model and do not change where it is saved.

Will Pro cost money?

Pro is the plan that keeps the Pro features open without a time limit. Its pricing has not been announced yet and will be published on this page. If you would like it for your firm, tell us.

What do you collect about me?

Nothing from your own computer: no telemetry, analytics or advertising, and your local models are never sent to us. When you sign in, the service keeps your account and what you save to the cloud. The privacy policy says exactly what, and for how long.
