Closed forms

Where the answer can be written down.

Without shear deformation, both benchmark problems have exact solutions. Over axial loads from 100 to 900 kips, the program's moments and deflections agree with the beam-column closed forms within 0.00001%. Automated tests hold other results the same way: a fixed-ended beam whose support settles carries 12EIΔ/L³ and 6EIΔ/L², and a structure built in one stage reproduces the plain analysis exactly.

Automated tests

How a wrong check is caught.

Published examples cover particular cases. These rules apply to every check, and a build that breaks one fails its tests.

Every check names its clause

A design check written without the clause it comes from fails a test, so every ratio can be traced to the page of the standard.

Every line of the working is evaluated

Each line of a member's working that shows numbers is computed again from its own inputs and must give that step's result, so a line that drops a term or an input fails.

The working is the check

The equations you read in a member's detail are recorded by the design's own functions and held to the design's result to the last bit. There is no second statement of a check to fall out of step.

LRFD and ASD share one strength

Over more than 3,000 strengths across every section family, the ASD strength is the same nominal strength over its Ω where LRFD takes its φ, each factor from its own clause.

Equilibrium, every combination

A separate calculation sums each combination's loads and reactions, and the report prints the result. Reactions balance loads within a millionth of their size.

More than 1,700 automated tests

Across the analysis engine, the steel checks, the load combinations, the model and the program itself, and repeated with every build.

Questions

Is it ready for real work?

Can StruTrix Studio be used for real steel design?

Yes, within its scope. It is professional software for practising engineers: three-dimensional frames of linearly elastic members under static loads, analysed to first order or by the AISC 360-22 direct analysis method, with every steel member designed by LRFD or ASD. As with any program, the engineer stays responsible for the model, the loads and the results. Connections are designed separately for now.

Are its AISC 360-22 checks verified?

They are held to the answers AISC published: 46 strengths from the AISC Design Examples within 1.35%, 43 of them within 1%, and the AISC 360-22 Commentary's second-order benchmarks within 0.37%. This page sets each one beside the program's value, and automated tests repeat them with every build. They are RamisoftSolutions' own comparisons; AISC and ASCE have not reviewed or endorsed the program.

Does it generate load combinations?

Yes. The ASCE 7-22 combination generator writes the strength, allowable stress and service combinations, with notional loads. The line loading writes a transmission line's combinations by the NESC, ASCE MOP 74 or MOP 113.

Does it produce calculation reports?

Yes, free and with no watermark: a typeset PDF of the basis, the model, the loads, the combinations, the equilibrium, the reactions and every member's checks clause by clause. A member detail works every check equation by equation with its clause, in a window or as a PDF.

Does it work with BIM tools?

It exports to IFC 4.3 with true steel profiles, and to Revit 2025, 2026 and 2027 as native structural columns and framing through the Ramisoft add-in. Both are Pro features.

Does it design connections?

Not yet. Today it designs the members, and a seismic design reports the connections' required strengths. Connection design is in development as a Pro feature, down to every plate, bolt and weld by AISC 360-22 Chapter J and ANSI/AISC 358-22.

Which design codes does it cover?

United States standards: AISC 360-22, ANSI/AISC 341-22, the member side of ANSI/AISC 358-22, ASCE 7-22, ASCE/SEI 48-19, ASCE MOP 74 and MOP 113, and the NESC. Eurocode, AS/NZS and other national steel codes are not offered.

Is it a student or educational program?

No. It is the same professional program in every plan, made for practising engineers on real projects. The analysis and the steel design are free with no limit on model size or time, no watermark and no account, and nothing restricts commercial use.

How long has it been available?

StruTrix Studio is new: it reached the Microsoft Store on November 1, 2026, so it does not yet have a long record of use, and its evidence is published here for that reason. Its frame analysis engine and steel checks also ship in FoundaTrix Studio.

Whose comparisons these are

These comparisons are RamisoftSolutions' own. AISC and ASCE have not reviewed or endorsed StruTrix Studio. A published example checks the program on that example, and does not establish that a model represents a structure: the engineer stays responsible for the model, the loads and the results, which must be reviewed by a licensed engineer before they are relied on in a stamped submittal.
