Engineering use and professional responsibility

Strutrix Studio is an engineering analysis and decision-support tool, not a substitute for the independent judgement of a qualified professional. Its results must be reviewed and approved by a licensed engineer before they are used for design, a drawing, a submittal or construction.

Results depend on the information, assumptions, criteria and code provisions selected by the user: the geometry, the supports and releases, the sections and materials, the loads and their combinations, and the analysis chosen for each combination. The program analyses frames of linearly elastic members under static loads. Plates, walls and solids, dynamic and time-history response, material nonlinearity, wind and earthquake load generation for buildings, sag-tension calculations, connections and base plates are outside its scope; it designs steel members only, and a steel design is made only from combinations analysed by the direct analysis method. Support reactions handed to another program are the loads of the structure as analysed, and are for that program's engineer to review as any other loads.
