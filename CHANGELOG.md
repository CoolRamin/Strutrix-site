# What's new in StruTrix Studio

Every release of StruTrix Studio in the Microsoft Store, newest first. The same notes are on the website at
https://www.strutrix.com/whats-new/, which is the authoritative copy; this file mirrors it for readers of this repository.

## Version 1.105 (November 7, 2026)

Staged construction analysis, seismic design by AISC 341-22, and the support reactions your foundations and piles now read.

### New

- **Staged construction analysis** (Pro) — an addition to an existing structure, or a structure built in any number of stages, analysed as it is built: each stage's loads carried by the structure standing then, every later load by the whole. Analysed at once, the addition shares loads it never carried, and the existing members you most need to check are understated. [Why it matters](https://www.strutrix.com/#staged)
- **Seismic design by AISC 341-22** (Pro) — on its own Seismic Design tab: ordinary, intermediate and special moment frames, cantilever column systems, ordinary and special concentrically braced frames, eccentrically braced frames and buckling-restrained braced frames, dual systems included. Each member's role is found from the model; the capacity-limited analyses, the prequalified connections of ANSI/AISC 358-22 on the beam, the required strengths of the column bases, splices and connections, and what the construction documents must show. [See the seismic design](https://www.strutrix.com/#seismic)
- **ASCE 7-22's seismic requirements** — the equivalent lateral force written as earthquake load cases (the seismic weight, the period, the base shear and its distribution, the accidental torsion and the torsional irregularity), the combinations with overstrength, and each story's drift and stability coefficient.
- **Rigid diaphragms** — mark a level in the Named Elevations table and its joints move as one body in its plane.
- **Your reactions, read by your foundations and piles** (Pro) — FoundaTrix Studio and PileTrix Studio, in the same cloud job, read the support reactions a run saves with the structure, as footing loads or a pile's head loads, and take its latest saved version before every run. A Pro feature of those programs; it needs your own StruTrix Studio subscription or trial as well. [How it works](https://www.strutrix.com/#link)

### Improved

- With nothing selected, the view turns about the centre of the model, and a pan holds the point you pressed on under the cursor.
- The User's Manual is rearranged in seventeen sections, with an engineering notice, getting started in ten minutes, a section on verifying results, and pictured tutorials of a building, a transmission pole, a staged construction analysis and a seismic design.
- Selecting thousands of members, and setting their construction stage, takes seconds.
- Escape in a drop-down list closes it without changing its value.

In the Microsoft Store as version 1.1.5.0. A model holding construction stages or a seismic design is kept in the cloud.

## Version 1.076 (November 1, 2026)

The first release in the Microsoft Store.

### In this release

- **Any frame in 3D**, drawn with drafting precision or generated: building frames, trusses, portal frames and circular grids from a few dimensions; supports on springs, end releases, rigid offsets and links, tension-only and compression-only members.
- **Loads and combinations**: joint, member, projected, area and thermal loads, self-weight and imposed displacements, and the ASCE 7-22 combination generator with notional loads.
- **First-order or AISC 360-22 direct analysis**, chosen per combination, on every core of your computer, with an independent equilibrium audit of every combination.
- **AISC 360-22 steel design** by LRFD and ASD at points six inches apart, Optimise for the lightest passing section, net deflection checks, and a member detail that works every check equation by equation.
- **The calculation report**, free with no account; your firm's name and logo on it as a Pro feature.
- **Tapered tubes to ASCE/SEI 48-19** with the Pole and H-Frame generators, and **transmission line design**: conductors' sag and tension, insulator swing, NESC clearances and line security loads (Pro)
- **Export to IFC 4.3 and to Revit** as native structural framing (Pro)
- **Cloud projects** with version history and a recycle bin, and your own AI assistant creating structures and running what-if analyses on your computer, which only you apply.

In the Microsoft Store as version 1.0.76.0.
